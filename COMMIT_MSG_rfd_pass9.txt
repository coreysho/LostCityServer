The quest: inspect the plinth, ask Murphy about the seabed, take the
fishbowl helmet and the diving apparatus and wear both, and he lowers you
on the anchor chain. Pick kelp, open the crab pen, kill crabs for meat, and
bring a raw cod and a loaf down with you. Grind all four with a pestle and
mortar, press them into a raw fishcake, cook it, give it to the frozen
Pete. Climb the anchor to get back up.
