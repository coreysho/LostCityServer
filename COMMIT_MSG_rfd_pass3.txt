Recipe for Disaster: Freeing the Lumbridge Guide (pass 3 of ~11)

The dish is a cake and the cache says so without ambiguity:
100guide_flourdone, 100guide_milkdone and 100guide_eggdone are the three
ingredients of one, and the cooking skill already builds cake_tin +
pot_flour + bucket_milk + egg -> uncooked_cake -> range -> cake.

The three ingredients come from the three people in Lumbridge who would
have them - the Cook, and the two local farmers Gee and Donie. The gifts
are one-time, which is what the three *done bits are for.

Dead op fixed on the way: Gee (lumbridge_guide2_man) and Donie
(lumbridge_guide2_woman) are placed twice each around Lumbridge with
op1=Talk-to and had no handler anywhere in the repo - four npcs that did
nothing when clicked. Same class as Oronwen's Trade and the castle doors.
They now have ordinary farmer chatter outside the quest as well.

NOT used, and recorded rather than invented: 100guide_q1 (5 bits),
100guide_q2 (4), 100guide_tot (4) and 100guide_lastgame (3) describe some
repeated number game with a running total. What the rules were is not
recoverable - the ranges do not add up as a sum, there is no interface for
it, and no npc carries a param that would drive one. Left at zero.

Sub-quests that need something from the Cook now get first refusal on his
conversation via a proc returning whether it did the talking, so pass 4
onwards can hook in the same way.

Not compiled and not playtested.
