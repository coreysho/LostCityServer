Recipe for Disaster: the frame (pass 1 of ~11)

RFD is ten quests in one. This builds the scaffolding every sub-quest
hangs off: the Cook, Aris, the way in and out of the frozen dining room,
the nine victims, the Culinaromancer's chest and the journal. Each of the
ten sub-quests is its own pass after this.

The cache ships essentially all of RFD - both dining rooms at m29_83
(frozen and finished), the Lumbridge portal and chest, BOTH shops at ten
tiers each (hundred_foodchest1..10 and hundred_rewardchest1..10, already
in inv.pack), the ten gauntlet levels and ten cooking weapons, and every
sub-quest's npcs, objs, locs and varbits. Nothing is blocked on missing
data.

hundred_subquest_tally is ten bits for ten sub-quests, which is also the
number of chest tiers, gauntlet levels and cooking weapons - so the reward
tiers unlock themselves off one counter. One quest point each;
~count_questpoints counts bits rather than comparing a stage.

Two shipped multis omitted the states the quest passes through, which made
it unstartable:
* hundred_aris listed states 3 and 4 only, and state 3 is set BY talking
  to her - so at state 2 she resolved to no child at all: no name, no
  model, no ops.
* hundred_portal_multi listed state 4 only. All three portal barriers are
  inside the frozen room and only lead out, so that left no way in during
  the intro and no way back after completion.

Standing bug fixed on the way, unrelated to this quest: the Lumbridge
Castle kitchen door and the dining-hall double doors have never opened for
anybody. They shipped with op1=Open, no category and no next_loc_stage,
so nothing in doors/scripts/ could handle them. Also fixed:
hundred_ilm_var_1 had no scope= line, so all of "Freeing King Awowogei"
would have been forgotten on logout.

Not compiled and not playtested - the build cannot run from the Linux side
of the device bridge (engine/node_modules/@esbuild is win32-x64 only).
