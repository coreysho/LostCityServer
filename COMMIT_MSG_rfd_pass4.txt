Recipe for Disaster: Freeing King Awowogei (pass 4 of ~11)

Every step is on or under Ape Atoll, so this one needs Monkey Madness -
nothing checks for it, because Ape Atoll's own gates already do.

The dish is named by the objs and needed no guessing: snake corpse +
sliced red banana + Tchiki nut paste -> "Raw stuffed snake" -> cook ->
"Stuffed snake / Fit for a Monkey King".

The three wise monkeys - Mizaru sees no evil, Kikazaru hears no evil,
Iwazaru speaks no evil - stand side by side at 0_43_43_35_43/36_43/37_43
and hold a third of the recipe each. All three had op1=Talk-to and no
handler.

The banana tree at 0_42_43_8_33 is a multiloc whose state 2 carries
anim=100_ilm_banana_tree_pull_down and no ops. That is the mechanic
stated in the data: the red ones grow at the crown, out of reach, and you
haul the crown down to get at them. Nothing in the cache reddens an
ordinary banana and nothing needed to.

Fixed on the way: hundred_ilm_snake ("Big Snake", level 84, three of them
in the pit at m47_85) had op2=Attack and full combat stats but NO
death_drop, so the one thing the sub-quest needs it for could not be
obtained. hundred_ilm_snake_corpse exists and nothing else drops or gives
it.

Also new: a cooking_generic dbrow for the stuffed snake. All three objs
were shipped but nothing had ever made the raw one cookable, so the last
step of the sub-quest had no way to happen. Adding the row is all it
needed - no new trigger.

NOT used, and recorded rather than forced: hundred_ilm_incorrectly_stuffed_snake
("Odd stuffed snake / Is this really what you wanted to do?"). Making one
would hand the player an obj with no iops at all - not even Drop - and no
way to undo it. The wrong-ingredient cases refuse instead.

Shared triggers extended rather than duplicated: [opheldu,knife] in
gnome_cooking/cutting_fruit.rs2 and [opheldu,pestle_and_mortar] in
quest_deserttreasure/scripts/dt_blood.rs2.

Not compiled and not playtested.
