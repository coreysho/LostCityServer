Recipe for Disaster pass 10: Defeating the Culinaromancer

Recipe for Disaster is finished. Ten sub-quests, ten quest points, the
Culinaromancer's Chest and all ten pairs of gloves.

He has been standing in the room the whole quest. culinaromancer_froze is
placed at 0_29_83_7_5, inside the frozen dining hall, with walkanim and
readyanim both 100_cul_froze and no ops at all - mid-spell, unclickable,
three tiles from where the player lands on their first visit. Left exactly
as he is.

THE BIG FIX: all eleven of the finale's combat npcs shipped with op2=Attack,
a vislevel and nothing else. No hitpoints, no attack, strength or defence,
no damagetype, no anims. Eleven unkillable bosses - the same defect
100_jubbly_bird had in pass 5. The anims were never the problem:
agrith_nana_*, rock_*, dessourt_* and horror_dagannoth_* all ship complete
attack / block / death sets.

The gauntlet is sequential and the shells say so. hundred_culinaromancer_
book5..book1 are five copies of the wizard, vislevel=hide, no ops,
readyanim=book_chuck_ready, each with a different model9 - the stack of
books in his hands, one fewer each time. Five books, five minions. After the
fifth he becomes hundred_culinaromancer_final, which is op2=Attack and
holding obj_hundred_cleaver_manwear - a cleaver, which is why he is built as
a melee fighter and not a caster.

The Gelatinnoth Mother's six forms are the one place the numbers carry a
mechanic. The cache names them air / water / fire / earth / melee / ranged,
which is the original saying "hit it with the matching thing", so each form
now has a defence of 5 against its own style and 1000 against everything
else. black_knight_titan already uses 1000 that way, so this is the repo's
own idiom rather than an invention. She cycles on a 30-tick ring.

SIMPLIFICATION, recorded in quest_100.constant: the engine's defence params
separate melee, ranged and magic and nothing finer, so the four elemental
forms all come out as "hit it with magic". Horror from the Deep solves the
finer version by special-casing the npc type inside the three combat
resolution files; that route is available if it is ever worth tightening.

Also fixed: hundred_rewardlamp, the quest's own reward, carries iop1=Rub and
its generated stub said "Nothing interesting happens." The genie lamp's
interface does the right thing and only needed to stop hardcoding which lamp
it consumes, so xplamp.rs2 now removes whichever one the player is holding
and bank.rs2 refuses to bank the RFD one too.

Every boss death hands off to a player queue rather than writing the tally
from the death hook - trap 4, the Horror from the Deep pattern. Nothing
calls npc_setmode; the bosses find the player through huntmode. The arena is
not instanced, which matches every other scripted quest boss in this repo.
