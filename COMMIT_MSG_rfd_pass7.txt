Recipe for Disaster: Freeing the Goblin generals (pass 7 of ~11)

Second correction in two passes: this quest's dish IS named too. The
prefix is 100goblin_ with no underscore after the 100, which is why it
never turned up under 100_ or hundred_:

    100goblin_compromise_mush  "Slop of compromise - Two out of two
                                goblin generals prefer it!"
    100goblin_soggy_bread      "Previously a nice crispy loaf of bread.
                                Now just kind of icky."
    100goblin_spicey_maggots   "They clearly taste so much better this way!"
    100goblin_dyed_oranges     "Orange slices which have been dyed, but it
                                looks more like they died."

Three things neither general will give up, mashed into one bowl. Every
input is an ordinary obj the repo already produces: bread, a bucket of
water, fishing bait, spice (which hundred_foodchest1 stocks), orange
slices from the gnome cocktail set, and any of Aggie's dyes.

The kitchen is under Goblin Village and the cache built it twice, the way
it built the dining room twice: intact at m46_154 p0 z50-62 with the
cauldron at (36,54), and a blasted copy at z15-22 with its own charred
ladder. Every fitting in the room has a _blasted or _charred twin.

The cauldron says what happens without being told - "The bottom of this
cauldron is suprisingly blackened, even for a cauldron" -> "I don't think
Cauldrons are supposed to bounce around like that" -> "Explosion - This
could be considered to be a bad thing!" - and 100goblin_explosion then
blows the roof off the hut above, in the village.

Shared triggers extended rather than duplicated: [opheldu,bucket_water],
[opheldu,spicespot], [opheldu,orange_slices] (which forwarded everything
to the cocktail system, so the dyes are checked before that fall-through),
the four dyes in dye_cape.rs2, and both generals' existing [opnpc1].

Caught by the battery before delivery, not by the compiler: an @label
inside [proc,hundred_goblin_chat] - trap 5b again, and the check added
after Mourning's End is what found it - and a sound_synth naming a synth
that does not exist.

Not compiled and not playtested.
