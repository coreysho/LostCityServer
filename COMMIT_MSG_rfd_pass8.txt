Recipe for Disaster pass 8: Freeing Evil Dave

The sixth of the ten sub-quests, and the most complete one in the cache -
Doris, the trapdoor, the cellar, the living Dave, eighteen Hell-Rats, the
Spicy stew with its unused Smell option, four spice shakers in four doses
each, eight dose/target varbits, a hell-rat catch counter, a slot to
remember what colour your cat was, and hell-cat forms for all three cat
stages. Nothing in the repo read or wrote any of it.

The quest: inspect the plinth, find Doris in Edgeville, go down her
trapdoor, and ask Evil Dave what he was eating. He wants an ordinary stew
with three doses of spice in it and will not say which. Take a cat down;
it becomes a hell-cat; Chase-vermin on the Hell-Rats; every fourth catch
brings out a Hell-Rat Behemoth, which drops a spice shaker. Shake spice in,
let Dave taste it, adjust, take the right one to the plinth.

One thing invented, because the cache does not have it: the Hell-Rat
Behemoth npc (configs/quest_100.npc, id 3858), reusing the hell-rat's own
model at twice the size. It is what the counter counts up to.

Two latent defects fixed on the way, neither of them this quest's:

  * op5=Chase-vermin was missing from all eighteen pet cats, so
    [opnpc5,_petcat] in quest_fluffs/scripts/pet.rs2 - a complete, sourced
    implementation of cats hunting vermin - had never been reachable by
    any player. Turned on for all eighteen.
  * The three hell-cat npcs and their three objs shipped with no category
    and no params at all, so the pet system could not see them and a
    picked-up hell-cat could never have been put back down.
  * 100_dave_celler_stairs, the only way out of the cellar, had an
    [oploc1] in stairs.rs2 with nothing but a default case.
  * hundred_dave_hellrat had no respawnrate, so the eighteen rats were
    gone for good once a cat had caught them.

One deliberate deviation, documented in quest_100.constant: the living
Dave gives per-colour feedback on a wrong stew. The target is still rolled
per player and still hidden. Three unknowns in 0..3 with no feedback is a
brute-force search, and the cache put a Smell option on the stew precisely
so the player can see where they are.

Also: param=next_obj_stage added to the sixteen spice shakers so the dose
chain reads a param instead of sixteen hardcoded pairs, and the journal
now lists Pirate Pete alone as the sub-quest still unwritten.
