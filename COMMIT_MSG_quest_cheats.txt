Add quest stage cheats so the untested backlog can be tested

A large amount of quest content has accumulated without ever being run:
Fremennik Trials, Recruitment Drive, Regicide's second half, Roving Elves,
Mourning's End Part 1 and all three Monkey Madness stages. A clean build only
proves it parses; the bottleneck is that testing any step means playing the
quest from the beginning.

_test/scripts/cheats/cheat_quests.rs2 adds ::mm, ::viking, ::rd, ::regicide,
::roving, ::mourning and ::dt, each taking a stage number and setting that
quest's varp plus its journal entry. ::mm also hands over the items each stage
assumes you are already carrying - royal seal and orders from stage 1, mould
and dentures from 6, the M'speak amulet from 8, talisman 9, greegree 10, zoo
monkey 12, sigil 14 - and ::mmtp jumps to the six coordinates the quest lands
you on.

Two things worth knowing for anyone extending this. A debugproc is handed NO
pointers, so each one acquires with p_finduid and does its work inside the
success branch rather than the early-return shape iku/ikd use. And RS2 cannot
write a varp named by a variable, so the per-quest varp assignment has to live
in each debugproc; only the journal update and the message are shared.

Verified against the tracked packs: all questlist components, objs, varps and
constants resolve.
