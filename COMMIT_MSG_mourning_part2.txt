Mourning's End Part I: build the second half (stages 5-20)

The quest now completes. Two of the three blockers recorded in
claude/mournings-end-part-1.md were wrong and are corrected there:

* "The HQ is a dead end" - the earlier pass read m39_51 p1, the upper
  floor. mourning_hideout_trap_door [8783] is on p0 at (46,63), in the
  walled yard, and both staircases on the route are already handled in
  stairs.rs2. Neither the trapdoor nor mourner_hideout_ladder1 had a
  handler; both do now.
* "The plague sheep are placed nowhere" - plaguesheep_1..4 are placed
  three to a pen in four pens on m40_52 and each is a multi-npc keyed on
  its own sheep varbit. Dyeing a flock is one varbit write.

Still true: there is no crosshair interface, so the sheep-dyeing is the
mitigation the old doc proposed (use the loaded device on a sheep), not
the real minigame.

New: mourning_hideout.rs2, mourning_toxin.rs2, mourning_gun.rs2,
mourning_elena.rs2.

Fixes found on the way:
* The four dyed plague sheep shipped with no ops at all, so dyeing a
  flock stripped its Prod option and made Sheep Herder unfinishable for
  that player. op1=Prod added to all four.
* mourning_quest_part2 had no scope= line, so the whole of Part II's
  state would have been forgotten on logout.
* mourning_orchard_applepile had no handler, which also left Biohazard
  with no in-world source of rotten apples.

Shared triggers were extended rather than duplicated: the four dyes in
dye_cape.rs2, regicide_barrel_naphtha in regicide_bomb.rs2, the cauldron
in quest_biohazard.rs2, and the four plaguesheep in diseased_sheep.rs2.

Not compiled and not playtested - the build cannot run from the Linux
side of the device bridge (engine/node_modules/@esbuild is win32-x64
only).
