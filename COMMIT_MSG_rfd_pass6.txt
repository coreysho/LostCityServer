Recipe for Disaster: Freeing Sir Amik Varze (pass 6 of ~11)

The doc had this one down as "no named dish obj". That was wrong - the
dish is named, it just does not say "Varze" in it:

    [hundred_fruit_blast]
    name=Dirty blast
    desc=A cool refreshing fruit mix. With ash in for some reason.

A Fruit Blast with ashes stirred through it. Both halves are ordinary objs
the repo already produces - the gnome cocktail system makes fruit_blast
and ashes come off any fire - so nothing here is invented. The only thing
the data left open was who tells you, and the answer is the man himself:
the ordinary sir_amik_varze at 2_46_52_16_8 in Falador castle, whose
existing [opnpc1] this hooks into rather than redeclaring.

The shortest of the ten by a distance, and it carries no stage varbit.
Every other sub-quest has one; this one has only 100amikvisible, a single
bit. Rather than carve a stage out of 100intro's three spare bits (8-10)
for the sake of symmetry, it reads off what the player is holding - there
is no step here that can be half-done.

Not compiled and not playtested.
