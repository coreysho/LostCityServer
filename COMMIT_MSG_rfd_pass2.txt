Recipe for Disaster: Freeing the Mountain Dwarf (pass 2 of ~11)

The first of the ten sub-quests, and the template the other nine follow.

Rohak is at 0_44_154_49_20, in the dwarf camp at the east end of the
tunnel under White Wolf Mountain - the same tunnel Fishing Contest opens,
whose east exit lands eleven tiles away at 0_44_154_60_22.

He is two npcs, not one, and the cache wired the swap itself:
hundred_dwarf_dad_multi (0 -> "An old Dwarf", 1 -> Rohak) and
hundred_dwarf_drunk_multi (2 -> drunk Rohak with the dwarf_drunk anims).
At state 2 the first shell resolves to no child and vanishes.

Chain: inspect the plinth -> find Rohak -> he only ever baked one that
badly and he was drunk at the time -> a dwarf will not drink a stranger's
ale without a coin in the bottom (which is what the shipped
hundred_dwarf_asgarnian_ale's own description says) -> drunk Rohak bakes
a red-hot rock cake -> quench it in any water source -> put it in the
frozen dwarf's hand.

hundred_inspect became a proc so a sub-quest can do work after printing
its plinth line; the eight unwritten victims still route through it with
$written = 0 and print the placeholder.

Shared triggers extended rather than duplicated: [oplocu,_watersource] in
general_use/scripts/water_sources.rs2 gained the rock-cake case.
[opheldu,coins] is newly declared - checked repo-wide first, nothing else
handles coins as a use-item and every handler it could have shadowed ends
in the same default message.

Not compiled and not playtested.
