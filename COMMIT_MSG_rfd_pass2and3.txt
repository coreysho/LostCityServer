Recipe for Disaster: the Mountain Dwarf and the Lumbridge Guide

Two of the ten sub-quests, in one commit because they share the hooks they
added to quest_100.rs2 and hundred_dining_room.rs2.

--- Freeing the Mountain Dwarf ---

Rohak is at 0_44_154_49_20, in the dwarf camp at the east end of the
tunnel under White Wolf Mountain - the same tunnel Fishing Contest opens,
whose east exit lands eleven tiles away.

He is two npcs, and the cache wired the swap itself:
hundred_dwarf_dad_multi (0 -> "An old Dwarf", 1 -> Rohak) and
hundred_dwarf_drunk_multi (2 -> drunk Rohak with the dwarf_drunk anims).
At state 2 the first shell resolves to no child and vanishes.

Chain: inspect the plinth -> find Rohak -> he only ever baked one that
badly and he was drunk at the time -> a dwarf will not drink a stranger's
ale without a coin in the bottom (which is what the shipped
hundred_dwarf_asgarnian_ale's own description says) -> drunk Rohak bakes
a red-hot rock cake -> quench it in any water source -> put it in the
frozen dwarf's hand.

--- Freeing the Lumbridge Guide ---

The dish is a cake and the cache says so without ambiguity:
100guide_flourdone, 100guide_milkdone and 100guide_eggdone are the three
ingredients of one, and the cooking skill already builds the rest. They
come from the Cook and the two local farmers, one gift each.

NOT used, and recorded rather than invented: 100guide_q1/q2/tot/lastgame
describe some repeated number game with a running total. What the rules
were is not recoverable - the ranges do not add up as a sum, there is no
interface for it, and no npc carries a param that would drive one.

--- Fixed on the way ---

Gee (lumbridge_guide2_man) and Donie (lumbridge_guide2_woman) are placed
twice each around Lumbridge with op1=Talk-to and had no handler anywhere
in the repo - four npcs that did nothing when clicked. Same class as
Oronwen's Trade and the Lumbridge Castle doors. They now have ordinary
farmer chatter outside the quest as well.

--- Structure for passes 4 onwards ---

hundred_inspect became a proc rather than a label so a written sub-quest
can set its stage after printing its plinth line; unwritten victims still
route through it with $written = 0. Each sub-quest owns a
[proc,hundred_<name>_journal] returning its own journal block. Sub-quests
needing something from the Cook get first refusal on his conversation via
a proc returning whether it did the talking.

Shared triggers extended rather than duplicated: [oplocu,_watersource]
gained the rock-cake case. [opheldu,coins] is newly declared - checked
repo-wide first, nothing else handles coins as a use-item and every
handler it could have shadowed ends in the same default message.

Not compiled and not playtested.
