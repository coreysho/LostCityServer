Fix unquoted comma in the jubbly cooking dbrow

Pack error: "Data invalid in row, double-check the reference exists" on
data=cantcookmessage_range - which reads like a missing symbol and was
nothing of the kind. .dbrow splits fields on commas, and I put one inside
a prose string, so the parser took " not a range." as an extra column.

Reworded to avoid the comma. The other option, which levelup.dbrow uses
throughout, is to quote the whole string:

    data=message,"Congratulations, you just advanced an attack level."

Checked every prose field in cooking_generic.dbrow and repo-wide for the
same thing; this was the only one.

Added to claude/rs2-compile-traps.md as trap 8 and battery item 13, with
the note that 12 and 13 are both config-format problems the .rs2 battery
cannot see - and both cost a build in this same quest.
